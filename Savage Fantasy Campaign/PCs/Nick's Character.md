A human noble from the city of [[Corsica]]. Played by Nick.

[[Nick's Character]] is minor nobility from the city of [[Corsica]]. The family he hails from is not extremely wealthy. Due to scandal, [[Nick's Character]] has moved to the larger city of [[Srouis]], where the story of the campaign begins. 

Having lived among nobilty, [[Nick's Character]] is exceptionally charming. He is not a very strong fighter, instead relying on charm and influence to overcome obstacles. Aside from this, he is skilled in the rapier, and with horseback riding.
