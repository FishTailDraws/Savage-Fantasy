[[Neon's Character]] is a skilled thief operating in the city of [[Srouis]]. 

Having lived as a street urchin, [[Neon's Character]] excels in the dexterous arts of stealth and thievery. Despite his training, he still has a heroic heart. As a [[Beastfolk]], specifically a [[Felid]], he often faces discrimination from the prideful [[Arcopolds]]. 

[[Neon's Character]] has learned to scoff at misfortune, being highly skilled in taunting and agility. He is exceptionally slippery. His "chances" with fate aren't much of a risk to him at all.
