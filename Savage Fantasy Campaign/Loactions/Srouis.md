A bustling city that serves as the opening location for the [[Savage Fantasy]] campaign.

# A Stain Among the Trees
Srouis is a large city located towards the south of the country of [[Arcopoli]]. It is a large center of industry, polluting the wide [[Hessen River]] that runs along its western side.

The city is known for its export of lumber. Being farther south, and close to the border, [[Srouis]] has become an important hub for the trade of lumber and manufactured goods. As a side effect of heavily exporting downstream, the city has thoroughly polluted the river. 

The wild forests surrounding the city makes it difficult to sustain agriculture. The soil around the city was used for slash and burn farms, which means that as time progressed food supplies became unstable. Most of the food available is imported, which raises the price.

# The Five Hearts of Srouis
The city is made of five boroughs, known as the Five Hearts. Each of these has a fairly distinct culture. 

## Heart of Wood, Eelward
Eelward is the west-most section of the city, and is where most industry takes place. Most of the shipping lanes and factories are found here. The place is populated by sweat shops and smaller, self insulated "company communes." These are essentially prisons used to keep employees reliant on the shipping companies for food and home.

## Heart of Gold, The Golden Nest
North of Eelward is a a wall that seperates it from the Golden Nest. This is the section of the city that houses most of the elites and wealthy merchants of the city. Known for its love of art and culture, most commoners only hear about this part of the city. The only sections made available to the public are the [[Floating Carnival]]. 

## Heart of Silver, Aldtown
Once a smaller city that became engulfed by Srouis, Aldtown is the Southernmost section of the city. It is home to the most fertile portions of farmland. It also serves as the hub for most skilled craftsmen. This section is a lot less densely populated than the others. Where it borders the river and other boroughs, entertainment and merchant districts have spung up, making it the "Poor Man's Nest."

## Heart of Ruby, The Sunder
This portion of the city is the largest. It is famously home to the city's red light district, the [[Devil's Tooth]]. One of the poorer parts of the city, it houses many of the people to travel for work at Eelward, or home to displaced families from Plague Ward.

Some industry exists here. The only reason a high class citizen would pass through here, however, is to check on their farming estates in Aldtown. This part of the city is infamously run by the [[Scarred Eye]] crime syndicate. 

This is also the part oft he city that [[Savage Fantasy]] begins in.

### The Adventuring Guilds
There are two factions attempting to keep peace in the city. The [[Almond Branch Guild]] is an independent adventurer's guild that serves the people directly. They see the [[Pheonix Guard]], the order of knights that serves as the official guard and police force, as corrupt. Meanwhile, the guard sees the Almond Branch as unprofessional and too idealistic to face the Scarred Eye.

## Heart of Stone, Plague Ward
In the past, this borough was used to contain citizens who had plague or leprosy. Now, it houses the poorest and "undesirable" peoples of the city. This area is less densely populated and has access to some farmland, though nowhere as good as that found in Aldtown. 

This part of the city has the highest percentage of [[Beastfolk]] living within. As more move to Srouis in search of employment, many are doomed to live in this forlorn district.

The Heart of Stone is a battleground between the Scarred Eye and Almond Branch, as neither has claimed complete dominion there yet.
